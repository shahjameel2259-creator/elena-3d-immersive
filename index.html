The Promise of Elena

In the heart of Hyderabad, but away from its hustle, lies Elena. Two towers built on a simple truth: it is possible to stay connected with yourself, and all the things you love, in a world of clutter and chaos.

The pause is not an escape. It is a return. Every inch of Elena, every nook and corner, is designed to celebrate it. Meditation rooms, quiet reading nooks, lotus ponds, butterfly gardens. A home that asks you to slow down, and gives you the space to.

The Pause

Nature · Water · Mind

Slow living, by design

Highlights of the Elena life

The pause is a lifestyle philosophy. It is about savouring each moment, being fully present, having meaningful conversations, and finding joy in simple pleasures. Elena was built to make all of that easy.

For the calmer mind

Meditation rooms, quiet reading nooks and mindfulness spaces. Indoor plants and a calming palette throughout.

For the fitter body

Walking, cycling, swimming, squash, basketball, yoga. Nothing is missing here.

For the youthful spirit

Spaces for contemplation and prayer, cookouts with neighbours, art installations, lotus ponds and butterfly gardens.

For the happier family

Plenty for the young, plenty for the elderly. Social activities and communal spaces that make Elena one big family.

    Reclaim work-life balance

    Kokapet's position on the Outer Ring Road keeps Hitech City, Gachibowli and the Financial District close. Mornings become less rushed, evenings are spent reconnecting with loved ones instead of battling traffic.

    More time for what matters

    World-class schools nearby, including Oakridge International and The Shri Ram Universal. Renowned hospitals such as Continental and AIG. The freedom to focus on leisure, family and the quiet beauty of Kokapet Lake.

    A life less hurried

    Step away from the city's chaos without surrendering its conveniences. A fine meal at Zega, The Sheraton, or at Mrs. Mage, The Hyatt. The latest attractions in Gachibowli and Hitec City, all minutes away, with a peaceful retreat waiting at the end of the drive.
